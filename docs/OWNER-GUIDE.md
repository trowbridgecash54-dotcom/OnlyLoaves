# OnlyLoafs — Owner's Guide

Everything you need to run the website, without touching any code. You do it all from your **dashboard**: a private page only you can open.

## Signing in

1. Go to your website's address followed by **/admin** — for example `onlyloafs.vercel.app/admin`.
2. Enter your email and password, then **Sign in**.
3. When you're done, click **Sign out** (top right). On a shared computer, always sign out.

There's no "sign up" button anywhere. Your account was created for you. If you forget your password, ask whoever set up the website to reset it.

The dashboard works on a phone too. On a small screen the menu (Overview, Orders, Club…) scrolls sideways.

## Before you open for orders

When the website first goes live, **ordering is switched off**. Customers can look around, but the order and Club forms say you're not taking orders yet. This gives you time to finish setting up.

Open the **Checklist** page. It lists everything still missing: prices, ingredients, photos, your story, your Club policies, and your permit. When you're ready — and your cottage-food permit is approved — go to **Settings → Business details**, tick **Open ordering to the public**, and click **Save business details**.

## The overview page

The first page after signing in shows, at a glance:
- new order requests, Club requests waiting for you, active members, loaves on the menu, unanswered messages
- upcoming pickups
- the latest activity

Click any number to go straight to that list.

## Bread

**Bread** in the menu.

**Change a price:** click the loaf's name → type the price (just the number, like `12` or `12.50`) in **Price per loaf** → **Save changes**. Leave it empty and the website says "Price confirmed when ordering".

**Add a photo:** on the loaf's page, under **Photo**, click **Choose File** and pick the picture (a photo straight from your phone is fine; it's resized automatically). You'll see a preview. Write a few words in **Photo description** (for example "Round loaf with a dark, crackly crust") — this helps visitors who use screen readers. Then **Save changes**. Photos are trimmed to the same shape on every page, so keep the bread in the middle of the picture.

**Ingredients and allergens:** type them exactly as on your labels. Leave them blank until you're sure — never guess. While they're blank the website tells customers to ask you first.

**Sold out / available:** change **Availability**. Sold-out loaves stay on the website but can't be requested.

**Add a loaf:** **Bread → Add a loaf**, fill in the details, **Add loaf**.

**Order on the page:** **Display order** — smaller numbers show first.

**Homepage:** tick **Feature on the homepage** to show a loaf under "What's Baking" (the first three are shown).

**The Club:** untick **Club members can receive this loaf** to leave a bread out of the Club.

**Remove a loaf:** **Archive** hides it but keeps it so you can bring it back. **Delete** removes it for good (tick the confirmation box first). Old orders keep the loaf's name either way.

## Orders

**Orders** in the menu. Each order request shows who it's from, what they want, their preferred pickup date, notes, and links to email, call or text them.

**A request isn't an order until you confirm it.** The customer was told you'll be in touch. To handle one:

1. Contact the customer (use the **Email** or **Call** link) to confirm the bread, total, how they'll pay, and pickup time and place.
2. Change **Status** to **Confirmed** and click **Update order**.
3. As you go: **Preparing** → **Ready for pickup** → **Completed**. Or **Canceled**.

Changing the status doesn't send the customer anything — talk to them yourself.

Use the buttons along the top (**Open**, **New**, **Confirmed**…) to filter, the search box to find a name or reference, and **Sort** to order by pickup date.

**"Email not sent"** on an order means the email copy to your inbox failed. The order itself is safe — it's right there.

## OnlyLoafs Club

**Club** in the menu.

### New requests to join

When someone asks to join, they appear under **Requests to join**. Nothing has started and nothing has been charged.

1. Contact them and agree on: the price, how and when they'll pay, their bread, their pickup day, and your Club policies.
2. While you're talking, you can set **Status** to **In conversation** and keep notes.
3. When everything's agreed: choose the **Membership starts** date and **Pickup day**, tick **I've agreed the terms, payment and pickups with this customer**, and click **Approve membership**.

To turn someone down, set **Status** to **Decline** and click **Update**. Tell them yourself.

If you've set a **subscriber limit** and it's full, approving will tell you so.

### Tracking a member's loaves

Click a member's name. At the top you'll see this month's **Allowance**, **Fulfilled** and **Remaining**.

**When a member picks up a loaf:** choose the bread, check the date, and click **Mark one loaf fulfilled**. Do this once per loaf. Clicking twice by accident won't count it twice.

For example, with 3 loaves a month: after one pickup it shows 3 / 1 / 2. After the third it shows 0 remaining, and the website won't let you record a fourth by mistake. If they're *buying* an extra loaf, tick **Record as an extra loaf**.

**Made a mistake?** Under **Loaf history**, click **Void** next to the wrong entry. The record stays (for your history) but the loaf is given back.

Each month starts fresh. Unused loaves don't carry over unless you decide on a rule (see Plan and terms).

### Payments

The website never takes or charges payments. Under **Payment for this period**, choose **Paid** (or Waived / Refunded), add a note like "cash" or "Venmo", and click **Record payment status**. The members list shows who isn't marked paid this month.

### Pausing or canceling

On the member's page, under **Membership**, change **Status** to **Paused** or **Canceled** and click **Save membership**. Paused and canceled members get no loaves until you set them back to **Active**. Let them know yourself.

### Plan and terms

**Club → Plan and terms** (link at the top right of the Club page).

- **Price per month** and **Loaves included each month** — change these anytime. Existing members keep the price and loaves they were approved with; tell them yourself if their terms change.
- **Subscriber limit** — the most members you can bake for. Leave blank for no limit.
- **Accept new requests to join** — untick to close the Club form (the message below is shown instead).
- **Policies** — payment, renewal, pausing and canceling, missed pickups, unused loaves, refunds. Write each one in plain words once you've decided it. Anything left blank shows "We'll confirm this with you before your subscription starts." **Decide and publish these before taking members.**

## Pickup

**Pickup** in the menu.

- **Pickup days** — tick the days you do pickups. The order forms then only offer those dates.
- **Minimum notice** — e.g. `2` means a customer ordering on Monday can choose Wednesday at the earliest.
- **Book up to** — how far ahead people can choose.
- **General pickup area** — a neighborhood or town only. This is shown publicly.
- **Exact pickup address** — kept private. It is **never** shown on the website. Share it with customers yourself when you confirm.
- **Days off** — pick a date and **Block date** for holidays or vacations.

A customer's chosen date is always a *preference* until you confirm it.

## Messages

**Messages** in the menu shows what people send from the Contact page. Reply using the email link, then click **Mark resolved**.

## Settings

- **Business details** — the "open ordering" switch, your public email (optional — leave blank to hide it), Instagram link (paste the full link, e.g. `https://www.instagram.com/yourname`; leave blank to hide it), a short description, an **announcement banner** across the top of every page (e.g. "No bake the week of December 22"), and **required disclosures** for the footer.
- **Wording and photos** — the homepage headline and text, the Club wording, and the homepage and Club photos.
- **Our Story** — your own words about how OnlyLoafs started, why you bake, how the bread is made, and why customers might choose you, plus a photo. Anything left blank says "We're still writing this part."

### Required disclosures

Only add wording your permit actually requires, exactly as given (for example a "Made in a Home Kitchen" statement or a permit number). Don't add anything until it's confirmed.

## Email notifications

Every request is always saved in your dashboard. If email notifications are set up, you'll also get an email for each one, and you can reply straight from it. Setting this up needs a free Resend account and is done by whoever manages the website (see the README).

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A red message at the top of a page after saving | It says what needs fixing — for example a missing name or a photo that's too big. Fix it and save again. |
| "That photo is larger than 5 MB" / "isn't a JPEG, PNG or WebP" | Use a different photo, or take a screenshot of it and upload that. |
| "All of this period's loaves have already been recorded" | The member has had all their loaves this month. If they're buying an extra, tick **Record as an extra loaf**. If a loaf was recorded by mistake, **Void** it. |
| "You've reached your subscriber limit" | Raise the limit in **Plan and terms**, or leave the request in conversation until a spot opens. |
| "This membership isn't active" | The member is paused or canceled. Set them to **Active** first. |
| "Email not sent" on a request | The request is safe. Check your email setup, and contact the customer from the dashboard. |
| You're sent back to the sign-in page | You were signed out. Sign in again. |
| "That account isn't authorized" | You're signed in with an account that isn't an owner account. Ask whoever set up the website. |
| A change doesn't appear on the website | Refresh the page. Changes normally show straight away, and always within a minute. |
| "That didn't save — something went wrong on our side" | Wait a minute and try again. If it keeps happening, ask whoever manages the website. |
